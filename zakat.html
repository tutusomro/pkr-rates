Zakat Calculator (PKR)

Silver Nisab method (Hanafi). Zakat is 2.5% of your net wealth if it reaches the Nisab.

How Zakat is calculated

Zakat is 2.5% of your net savings and assets, if your wealth has stayed above the Nisab for one full lunar year.

The Nisab is the minimum amount. In the Hanafi view used in Pakistan it equals the value of 52.5 tola (about 612 grams) of silver. The gold Nisab is 7.5 tola (about 87.5 grams).

Include cash, bank savings, gold, silver, business stock and money owed to you. Subtract debts you must pay soon.
